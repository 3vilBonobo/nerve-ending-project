Optional developer / QA recording. Capture game questions, responses, actions and progression to help improve the game. Disabled until you opt in.

Your choice is remembered in this browser. Recording starts from the moment you enable it; earlier play is not collected. Turning it off keeps existing data.

LOCAL RETENTION & EXPORT

Recordings stay in this browser’s IndexedDB database nerve-ending-qa, across reloads and investigation restarts, until you clear QA data or site data. Your browser may also evict stored data. Export before changing browser or device.

Nothing is uploaded or used for training automatically. Export creates a readable .jsonl file in Downloads or your chosen save location. Each line is one event; sequence numbers preserve session order.

No account, passwords, authentication settings, tokens or device identifiers are collected. Common credential, email, phone and URL patterns in typed text are redacted before storage. Do not enter personal information or secrets in game questions or feedback; review exports before sharing.
