## Change classes and risk

**Content** means authored records, replies, labels and documentation. **Mechanics** means access rules, recognition/routing, encounters, operations or presentation behavior. **Persistence** means serialized identifiers, progress meanings, migration or telemetry contracts. A text edit can still affect disclosure, search or language recognition; content is not automatically low risk. Risk is relative: low = local wording with stable meaning/access, medium = several sources or reveal boundaries, high = progression, authority, ending effects or saved-state compatibility.

## What is safe once the full specification arrives

First reconcile the reviewed record manifest, disclosure matrix, causal timeline, scenario/scene graph, evidence/authority rules and ending truth table against A01–A06. Keep each unresolved field explicitly WORKING or OPEN; do not infer a solution from existing code. The author need not revisit already ESTABLISHED direction, but must supply its concrete expression and the remaining open facts.

With that specification and authorization, local wording corrections, neutral welcome changes, revised role descriptions and approved record additions can usually use the existing engine and UI. Keep stable identifiers/access meanings where possible, and test search/reveal effects even for content-only edits. No refactor, backend, generative dialogue model or new persistence format is inherently required.

Access changes, public-versus-clinical identity separation, new initiative scenes, treatment authority and route/ending behavior require a deliberate mechanics pass. Change save contracts only where new persistent progress or changed identifier meanings actually require it, with explicit migration and QA compatibility. Never treat an internal knowledge claim as permission to disclose or intervene.

Until then, the safe completed action is reference ingestion and this impact analysis. Runtime content, dialogue, examples, save schemas, state transitions and endings remain unchanged. Their existence and passing historical tests do not establish conformity to canon v0.3.
