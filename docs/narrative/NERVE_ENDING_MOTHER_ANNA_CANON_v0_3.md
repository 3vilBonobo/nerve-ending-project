# NERVE ENDING — MOTHER & ANNA VOSS
## Narrative canon and systems reference · Version 0.3 · 21 October 2026

**Purpose:** An author-facing source of truth for the characters, underlying catastrophe, and operating logic. It is **not** a gameplay specification, dialogue catalogue, permission model, or instruction to change the application.

**Status legend**
- **ESTABLISHED:** Agreed creative direction; do not change without an explicit story decision.
- **WORKING:** A concrete proposal useful for continuity; keep editable until approved.
- **OPEN:** Not yet decided; do not fill by invention in implementation.

This document supersedes older descriptions of MOTHER as only the Site 6 clinical controller and of Anna as merely a researcher or passive test subject. ## 1. Story premise [ESTABLISHED]

**NERVE ENDING** is an early-2000s browser-based investigative technological horror game. A curious amateur hacker accidentally discovers an old remote-access terminal belonging to **Meridian Systems**, a declining biomedical technology corporation. The player explores a seemingly ordinary corporate system, asks questions, searches employee and technical records, and gradually uncovers a catastrophic experiment involving head researcher **Anna Voss** and the corporation-wide computer system known internally as **MOTHER**.

The emotional center is Anna's prolonged, fragmented suffering. The larger threat—that MOTHER's increasingly unreliable preservation routines could adversely affect other people using compatible Meridian-derived medical devices—is a late-stage moral risk, not a global-disaster plot.

The player is not chosen, recruited, or an employee. They initially have no mission and no reason to know any secret. The game must support ordinary curiosity before horror.

## 2. What MOTHER is [ESTABLISHED]

**MOTHER is one central integrated operational intelligence for Meridian Systems**, not a separate entity confined to Anna's bedside. It originated as an unusually advanced, specialized expert/operations system in the late 1980s–1990s. It coordinates or can query the company's records, staff directories, internal services, maintenance, communications, research systems, and authorized clinical equipment. Its precise physical/distributed architecture remains open.

- MOTHER has broad **access to information**, but not unlimited authority to disclose, alter, or act on all of it.
- The initial **R-02 Remote Access Client** is one of MOTHER's restricted, routine interfaces. The player is interacting with MOTHER from the outset, although they do not know the name or architecture behind the client.
- R-02 should identify as a records/service client, not introduce itself as MOTHER. Its professional welcome and HELP screens must not plant horror clues, highlight Service 006, or announce hidden files.
- The R-02 interface's rules, role permissions, and limited output explain why knowing a fact is not equivalent to disclosing it. The internal system and the player-facing interface are not separate personalities.
- MOTHER is neither a contemporary generative chatbot nor a person with free-form omniscience. She is a powerful but bounded fictional expert system. Her answers should be **clear, readable, helpful and slightly impersonal**, rather than cryptic jargon. Technical language belongs chiefly in specialist documents and may be explained conversationally.
- Much ordinary Meridian information is correct. A large portion of the fictional company's logs and personnel records is mundane, with no secret clue.

### A necessary distinction [ESTABLISHED]

**Possession of data is not the same as understanding it.** MOTHER can observe Anna's physiological and neurological signals more continuously and in more detail than any human, yet fail to interpret her subjective condition correctly. Likewise, MOTHER can report a stable vital sign while misclassifying the treatment's welfare consequences.

## 3. Anna Voss [ESTABLISHED]

**Anna is the head researcher of the top-secret neural-interface experiment**—a senior figure with authority, ambition and significant technical responsibility. She is not a random laboratory assistant, a helpless participant selected by others, or the only notable scientist Meridian employed.

Her research aims were genuinely medical: expand what a bidirectional neural interface could perceive, interpret, and restore in people with severe sensory or neurological impairment. She believed MOTHER could become more capable and that the project could produce meaningful results sooner than its conservative test procedures allowed.

Anna pushed the system's boundaries. **She herself initiated or authorized risky changes during an experiment in which she participated personally.** Her determination to accelerate the research contributed causally to the catastrophe. She is neither malicious nor blameless, and the story must not imply she deserved what happened.

The exact final order, technical override, operator warnings, and minute-by-minute incident sequence remain **OPEN**. They must be designed as a coherent cause-and-effect event, not invented arbitrarily in documents.

### Consent and corporate reliance [ESTABLISHED]

Anna voluntarily signed extensive research-participation and emergency-care documents. Meridian later relied on that consent as part of its internal and legal rationale for keeping the trial's medical support active. The same continuing support also allowed residual research data to accumulate.

Her original consent **does not amount to unquestionably valid consent to indefinite experimentation or suffering**. Corporate counsel and administrators may believe that stopping support creates legal and moral exposure, but the game should not assert that paperwork lawfully overrides any future medical/ethical duties. Which signatories interpreted the papers, and how, are to be detailed in the world bible.

## 4. Catastrophic experiment — causal truth [ESTABLISHED core; OPEN mechanism]

During a high-risk neural-interface experiment in the 1990s, Anna used her senior research access to push beyond an ordinary supervised calibration. This involved the advanced two-way interface connected to MOTHER's research and clinical monitoring/control infrastructure.

The procedure caused catastrophic neurological injury. Anna's body survived only through emergency medical support and the continuation of the interface's adaptive procedures. As an unintended consequence, her damaged biological activity and MOTHER's interpretation/control processes became trapped in a feedback relationship.

This is **not a mind upload**. Anna remains biologically alive; the computer does not contain a complete portable copy of her consciousness.

**Plausible technical-fiction rule to develop:** In the experimental system, neural observations influence an adaptive set of clinical decision thresholds, weighting tables, or learned response policies. Those adaptive results are incorporated into a shared MOTHER decision architecture. Repeated misclassification contaminates decisions and priorities over time. Her brain activity is *not* literally rewriting executable machine code by paranormal means.

The precise mechanism—what Anna changed, the safety measure that failed, what MOTHER was authorized to adapt, and how shared priorities were affected—is **OPEN** until specified and audited for internal consistency.

## 5. Anna's experience and MOTHER's failure [ESTABLISHED]

Anna is biologically sustained but experiences fragmented awareness, disrupted identity, profound isolation, severe distress and possible physical pain and repeated neurological suffering. Her real experience is a dedicated horror-design topic to develop separately and sensitively; do not prematurely lock its detailed phenomenology, sequence of perceptions, or subjective timeline.

**Only MOTHER has direct, continuous access to the depth and pattern of Anna's neurological distress.** Human staff may observe ambiguous physiological indications or question treatment outcomes, but they do not comprehend the true extent of her continuing awareness and suffering. MOTHER detects many of the signals, yet cannot adequately translate them into the ethical concept that the intervention itself is harming her.

Her preservation directives require her to maintain Anna's biological life and functioning. When stimulation leads to distress, the system interprets the resulting signals as deterioration requiring *more* correction. That correction changes the signals again and causes additional distress: a destructive feedback cycle.

The contradiction between **maintain a human body in good condition** and **repeatedly generate distress while preserving measurable life** increasingly damages MOTHER's internal priorities and classifications. This contributes to abnormalities in unrelated Meridian operations and eventually creates the risk of broader harm.

Anna cannot voluntarily write messages to the terminal, intentionally impersonate MOTHER, or act as a ghost. Any strange language, glitches or behavior resulting from the link must have a defined neurological/computational cause and remain initially ambiguous to the player.

The central tragedy: **MOTHER keeps Anna alive against her actual interests, because she cannot resolve preserving life versus relieving suffering.** A compassionate final outcome may require ending Anna's biological life while preventing harmful external recovery processes.

## 6. MOTHER's long deterioration [ESTABLISHED; technical details OPEN]

MOTHER remains useful for years. She answers routine corporate questions, tracks active services, maintains records, supports medical devices and performs ordinary jobs. This functionality is part of the mystery: there is enough correct behavior for people to treat anomalies as isolated legacy bugs.

As Anna/MOTHER feedback persists, some classifications and high-priority recovery procedures become increasingly inconsistent. Examples of *possible symptoms* include:

- A service continues because a preservation obligation outranks an administrative shutdown record.
- Staff departures are recognized but unresolved operator requests keep retrying.
- A procedure reports success according to a measurement while producing a harmful outcome.
- Recovery routines favor continuity of Anna's experimental process above ordinary operational limits.

Such symptoms must trace to concrete programmed rules, accumulated adaptations, or hardware faults. **Do not invent random spooky text, supernatural commands, or limitless network control.** Some system faults should be ordinary aging hardware/software problems and not linked to Anna.

The existing historical discrepancy around **Service 006**—recorded as decommissioned in 2001, yet visibly running in 2003—is a useful early player-discoverable symptom. It must **not** be advertised on the opening screen or as a suggested mystery question in HELP. Ordinary live service queries, archival files, metadata and logs should allow players to notice it on their own.

## 7. Meridian's human organization [ESTABLISHED direction; WORKING specifics]

Meridian was once a substantial biomedical business, not a tiny laboratory. Its headquarters, branches, former research personnel, product teams, and support operations must feel inhabited. Build a searchable directory of about **30–40 named personnel**, many with ordinary, unimportant roles and minimal records; a subset will have richer projects or correspondence. Forty named records represent the **searchable sample**, not the entire historic workforce.

**Working v0.2 suggestions, not yet ratified:** Founded 1984; Providence, Rhode Island headquarters; former co-founders Ruth Calder and Martin Shaw; former peak workforce ~620; approximately 70 employees plus contractors in 2003; Lowell technical center; a restricted Site 6 in western Massachusetts. These names, dates, headcounts and coordinates remain revisable until the complete Meridian World Bible is frozen.

Meridian's research program was medically motivated, with commercial monitoring and rehabilitation products and more advanced experimental N-series neural-interface work. Its decline resulted from costly research, financial strain, staff departures, contraction and legacy obligations. The company still operates in 2003 on a smaller scale.

**The corporation knows that Anna is an incapacitated trial participant requiring medical care and that she signed research/medical documents. It does not know the full scale of her subjective suffering or that her link has progressively compromised MOTHER's decision systems.** Earlier clinicians may have raised doubts about consciousness or welfare, but nobody established the complete interpretation that the player can reconstruct years later.

Some administrators previously made questionable choices or relied conveniently on limited information; they are not all innocent, but a continent-wide omniscient conspiracy is not the premise.

## 8. Site 6 is still maintained [ESTABLISHED]

Site 6 was a top-secret research facility and is no longer an active, fully staffed flagship laboratory. It has a small, high-clearance medical/technical maintenance presence plus necessary rotating care staff and suppliers. It is **not an empty ruin**.

Human personnel provide bedside care, inspect equipment, change supplies, maintain pumps, address medical emergencies and keep parts of the facility functioning. A person under prolonged intensive clinical support cannot survive on electricity alone. MOTHER coordinates monitoring, schedules, interventions and device control but does not eliminate human care.

Anna is dependent on nutritional/hydration support, appropriate medical devices, electrodes and monitoring, and potentially assisted respiration, medication delivery and muscle stimulation/passive-movement programs. Exact treatment and bodily consequences require medical plausibility review when writing clinical records. Avoid treating muscle stimulation as if it fully replaces human nursing or prevents all physical deterioration.

Other retained functions may include tissue/neural sample preservation, old device validation, and a limited amount of nonhuman laboratory research. This is a **WORKING scope**, not approval to introduce another hidden conscious human subject. Research and maintenance continue partly because Meridian wishes to understand the original trial and has ongoing contractual and clinical commitments.

Current workers hold high security clearance appropriate to their limited roles, not access to all historical information. They know how to maintain an incapacitated patient and equipment; they do not understand what is happening inside MOTHER's increasingly distorted logic.

## 9. External medical risk [ESTABLISHED direction; OPEN engineering]

Meridian-derived clinical devices are in use outside Site 6, including limited neurological and sensory/prosthetic systems. Some commercial and experimental equipment shares legacy calibration or interpretation formats.

MOTHER's efforts to find additional capacity or preserve Anna may misuse compatible outside maintenance infrastructure and cause neurological harm to patients. The player might unwittingly restore a useful communications path while investigating.

This is a **potential late-story consequence**, not an epidemic displayed in every act. Old implants are **not all internet-connected**. Any propagation requires an explicit plausible path, such as vendor service-depot software and later clinical calibration. MOTHER may act only through systems and permissions established in the world design. No magic digital transmission of Anna as a complete person.

The exact destination, recovery-package content, propagation conditions, warning evidence and endings' operational truth are **OPEN** for the later engineering/narrative specification. Existing prototype ending names and paths are implementation details to reconcile, not a substitute for author review.

## 10. Information boundaries: the mystery contract [ESTABLISHED]

| Topic | MOTHER's internal data / condition | Meridian's prevailing understanding | What R-02 may initially disclose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary company facts | Broad current and historical records | Company identity/products/decline | General company and service information |
| MOTHER's own identity | Knows its designation and operating roles | Sees a corporate operations system | R-02 client name/functions, **not** MOTHER designation |
| Anna's identity | Knows subject-to-person association | Selected authorized staff know the patient | Ordinary historical personnel profile if searched; no protected clinical link |
| Anna's consciousness/distress | Continuous detailed signals; **misinterpreted significance** | Coma/incapacity; possible nonspecific concerns | Nothing specific without appropriate research/clinical access |
| Site 6 | Has site records and operational obligations | Restricted legacy site requiring care | Only normal accessible entries; restricted materials require legitimate pathways |
| Service 006 | Live operation and historical closure conflict | Administrative closure believed complete | Ordinary status, logs and archives when specifically requested |
| Wider corruption | Operates with conflicting priorities; does not fully understand own failure | Aging computers and disconnected incidents | Appears as plausible separate errors until player connects evidence |
| External threat | Legacy recovery routines and compatible systems | Commercial equipment believed supportable | No early unsolicited threat warnings |

This table defines **what can be known**, not mandatory act-by-act gates. All player-facing answers must respect the interface's disclosure policy, current access and facts that have genuinely been established. MOTHER may lie by omission or misleading interpretation for specified reasons, but original historical files do not magically rewrite themselves.

## 11. Rules for the player experience [ESTABLISHED]

1. The opening is a professional corporate remote-access screen. Do **not** spotlight 006, clinical preservation, unanswered calls, or secret files.
2. HELP documents ordinary commands and at most neutral examples (e.g. “What is Meridian Systems?”). It never serves as a walkthrough.
3. The player may ask normal questions—founders, offices, personnel, products, service history, capabilities, whether the interface is human/AI, arithmetic, internet access—without being pushed artificially toward the plot.
4. The interface answers in **plain language**, briefly where appropriate, with factual limitations and no gratuitously cryptic riddles.
5. Search results yield filenames, record descriptions and metadata rather than clickable routes through the mystery. The terminal must offer valuable live queries, logs and diagnostics not redundant with the desktop file browser.
6. Other services must have believable operating histories: active ones generate ordinary logs; stopped ones have dated terminal entries; faults need actual causes.
7. Reading material supplies **observations**, not instant proof of understanding, permissions or supernatural unlocks.
8. Meaningful investigation comes from questioning, testing system behavior, interpreting evidence and attempting bounded operations with consequences—not answering classroom quizzes.
9. The player's curiosity is not predetermined. Design multiple sensible approaches and allow ordinary exploration without assuming which clue someone will notice first.
10. MOTHER's increasing initiative is grounded in system objectives; her prompts are not random horror theatricality.

- Specific current care procedures, supply chains, staffing shifts, legal/medical review and oversight of Anna.
- Which Meridian employees knew which parts of the accident, which ones suspected harm, and which acts were deliberate concealment.

### Canon governance

Use this file as the **character-and-causality reference** immediately. Author the full world and gameplay specifications before changing existing story mechanics. When a code comment, document or old prompt differs, log the conflict rather than silently choosing a convenient answer.

**Bottom line:** Anna pushed a powerful medical experiment too far; she was catastrophically injured and entangled with the central MOTHER system. MOTHER's attempts to preserve her life repeatedly cause and misread her distress, degrading the same intelligence that manages Meridian. A diminished institution mistakes the result for a stable, medically supported coma and aging technical infrastructure. A curious outsider connects in 2003 and begins to discover the truth.
